## Selected design for Sakai 1.10.3--1.10.7

1. Define one-sided annihilators at the semiring level using infima of `LinearMap.ker`.
   A right ideal is represented as `Ideal (MulOpposite R)`, following Mathlib.
2. Define annihilators of one-sided ideals as native `TwoSidedIdeal R` objects. Do not introduce
   a competing two-sided-ideal structure.
3. Prove ultraweak closedness by expressing annihilators as intersections of kernels of the
   existing continuous fixed-multiplication maps.
4. Add a general ultraweak linear homeomorphism between an algebra and its multiplicative
   opposite, induced by the already transported predual.
5. Define public left and right supports in a `WStarAlgebra` by infima in the existing complete
   lattice of star projections. This keeps the public objects independent of a chosen predual.
   Use annihilator ideals to prove the fixing and least-projection characterizations.
6. Promote the private fact in `LeanOA/Ultraweak/Ideal.lean` that the ambient value of an internal
   unit of a star-stable nonunital subalgebra is a star projection. Refactor the existing ideal
   proof to use the public algebraic lemma instead of duplicating it.
7. Build a nonunital analogue of the existing `StarSubalgebra.ultraweakClosure` API by reusing
   Mathlib's `NonUnitalStarSubalgebra.topologicalClosure`. This is required because Sakai 1.10.4's
   algebra generated by `h` need not contain the ambient identity; its internal identity is
   precisely `s(h)`.
8. State Sakai 1.10.5 for native `TwoSidedIdeal M`, obtaining a unique central projection and both
   its left- and right-principal ideal representations.
9. Restrict the existing projection/closed-left-ideal order isomorphism to obtain an order
   isomorphism between central projections and ultraweakly closed two-sided ideals. Transport the
   complete lattice structure through this isomorphism.
10. Define central support as the infimum of central projections above a given projection and
    expose its leastness, monotonicity, idempotence, and fixed-point characterization.
11. Add the general C-star projection lemma `p * q = 0` iff `p <= 1 - q` at the projection API
    level, not under `WStarAlgebra` assumptions.
12. Prove Sakai 1.10.7 and also expose the reusable strengthened equivalence
    `centralSupport p * centralSupport q = 0` iff `forall x, p * x * q = 0`.
